Research date: 2026-11-13. Part D of the Baghbaan research appendix (follows A, B and C in this folder). It covers the spray schedule, nutrition, harvest and storage, the pest and disease library, input quality and rules, labour and costs, and what is still open.

The verifier parsed the same copy. Check every row against the printed booklet (district horticulture offices) before release. The app must show "IMG 2026" on every recommendation drawn from it.

## How to read this document

Every fact has a tag and a source link.

- **[V]**: confirmed by the independent verifier at the source (or at a better source, which is then the one linked).
- **[S]**: the researcher read it at the source, but the verifier could not open or confirm it. Check before relying on it.
- **UNVERIFIED**: known only from a search-engine summary, or the verifier marked it uncertain and nobody read it at source. Treat it as a lead.
- **Corrected**: the verifier refuted the researcher's claim. Only the corrected fact is given, with the verifier's source.
- **Analysis**: my own reading or recommendation, not a sourced fact.

Doses are given **only** where a fact is tagged [V] or [S]. SKUAST gives doses per **100 L of water**; any per-drum (200 L) figure would be our own doubling, not a SKUAST figure [V] ([IMG 2026](https://www.scribd.com/document/1002728111/Apple-Spray-Schedule-2026-SKUAST-K)).
